Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Household Bills is a personal, non-commercial tool. It connects only to its owner's own dedicated Gmail account and is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

The tool reads messages in that mailbox, applies labels, archives or trashes messages, and extracts bill amounts, dates and account credits for a private household expense ledger.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed only on the owner's own computers. OAuth tokens are stored locally in files readable only by the owner. Bill text may be reviewed by a language model running on the owner's own hardware; it is not sent to outside AI services. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Locally stored data is deleted when the tool is removed.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the mailbox owner through the Gmail account this tool is connected to.
